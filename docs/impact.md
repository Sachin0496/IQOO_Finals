# Impact: who loses their voice, and what they have today

*7 Oct 2026, Sachin. Every number has a source below; estimates are labelled. Groups overlap, so **never add the rows
up**: say "at least" and quote the largest single source.*

**Mouna's one job:** give a voice back to anyone who has lost theirs, whatever the cause. They teach Mouna with
whatever still moves (lips, a half smile, an eye look) and Mouna speaks for them, offline, in the caregiver's
language. No screening, no diagnosis, nothing else.

## How many people

| Who | Number | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **People worldwide who cannot rely on speech** to communicate | **> 97 million** | Penn State / J. Light, AAC research (autism, CP, ALS, stroke, TBI, dementia…) [1] |
| **Indians with a speech disability** (Census 2011) | **about 20 lakh** (7.5% of 2.68 crore people with disabilities) | Census 2011, MoSPI statistical profile [2] |
| **Indians living with aphasia after a stroke** | **about 20 lakh**; 21–38% of stroke survivors | Pauranik et al. 2019 [3] |
| Stroke survivors with dysarthria (slurred, weak speech) | 52% of inpatients had dysarthria (28% with aphasia, 24% alone) | Manchester inpatient study [4] |
| **People with ALS / MND** who cannot meet daily needs by speech | **80–95%** | USSAAC / ALS speech studies [5] |
| **People with Parkinson's** with speech or voice disorders | **89%**; India: 5.8 lakh (GBD, conservative) to 70 lakh (clinician estimate) | [6], [7] |
| **Children with cerebral palsy** | 2.95 per 1,000 children in India; ~20% non-verbal, 82% with disordered speech | Indian meta-analysis [8], [9] |
| **Laryngeal and hypopharyngeal cancer** (new each year, India) | **about 70,000** | GLOBOCAN 2024 (docs/submission.md) |
| **Voiceless ICU patients** (ventilator, tracheostomy) | 49% extreme difficulty communicating; **90.7% felt trapped** | docs/question-everything.md, Part B |

## What they have today

| Gap | Number | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Speech therapists in India | **about 2,500** registered audiologists + speech therapists (ISHA) for 140 crore people; 48% of post-graduates leave the country | [10] |
| Eye-gaze speech devices | **$4,000–23,000** (Tobii Dynavox range), imported, English-first | [11] |
| In the ward | a whiteboard, pointing, or nothing | docs/question-everything.md |
| Cloud lip reading (SRAVI) | English only, video sent to a server; 22–35% in UK critical care | docs/submission.md |

## Lines for the pitch (from the numbers above)

- *"Over 97 million people worldwide cannot rely on their voice. In India, about 20 lakh have a speech disability, and
  another 20 lakh lost words to a stroke. They share about 2,500 speech therapists."*
- *"The devices that speak for them cost as much as a car. Mouna is an app on the phone they already have."*
- *"Nine in ten voiceless patients feel trapped."*
